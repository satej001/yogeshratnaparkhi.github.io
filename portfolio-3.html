Investment Plans & Services

Smart ways to invest, withdraw and grow your money.

📈

SIP – Systematic Investment Plan

Invest a fixed amount regularly in mutual funds. Build discipline, benefit from rupee-cost averaging and let compounding grow your wealth over time.

💰

SWP – Systematic Withdrawal Plan

Withdraw a fixed amount at regular intervals from your investment — ideal for a steady monthly income during retirement or for regular expenses.

💼

Lumpsum Investment

Putting a large or single amount of money into financial assets at once, for security and faster wealth creation.
